## Contact Us

We would love to hear from you. Whether you have feedback on the **AI watermark detector**, a feature request, a bug report, or a partnership inquiry, here is how to reach us.

## Email

For all inquiries — support, feedback, security reports, business, press — please email:

**`tnga86892@gmail.com`**

We aim to respond within two business days.

## Bug reports

When reporting a bug, please include:

1. The browser and operating system you are using.
2. A short description of what you expected and what actually happened.
3. The exact text (or a representative sample) that triggered the issue, if applicable.
4. Any error messages you saw.

Please redact any personal or sensitive information from the text you share — by default, your text never leaves your browser, but anything you email us obviously does.

## Feature requests

We are actively building the next version of Detect AI Watermarks. Top of the list:

- Image metadata remover (EXIF stripping).
- Text Watermark Remover as a standalone tool page.
- Batch file processing.
- API access for developers.
- User history and export (only for signed-in users who opt in).

If you want a specific feature, email us and we will prioritize accordingly.

## Privacy reminders

- Please do not send us the full text of any document you would not share publicly.
- If you are reporting an academic-integrity case, you do not need to send us the student's text — the detection report from your own browser is sufficient context.
- For security disclosures, please use email and we will respond within one business day.

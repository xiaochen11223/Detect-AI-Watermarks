## About Detect AI Watermarks

**Detect AI Watermarks** (`detectaiwatermarks.com`) is a free, browser-first AI watermark detector that scans your text for hidden Unicode characters and lets you clean and copy the result in a single click. It is built for writers, editors, students, teachers, developers, and anyone who ships text to readers, screens, or systems.

## Why we built it

In 2025, AI-generated text is everywhere — and so are the invisible Unicode characters it carries. Zero-width spaces, variation selectors, directional marks, and BOM characters leak from AI tools into CMS pipelines, database inserts, screen readers, and search indexes. They cause real, measurable damage: broken layouts, failed JSON inserts, screen-reader bugs, and homoglyph spoofing in phishing.

We wanted a free, private, no-signup tool that would let anyone detect and remove these characters in seconds. No model inference, no API key, no "please log in with your Google account" gate — just paste, scan, clean, copy.

## What we detect

Our scanner covers 30+ invisible Unicode characters across the following categories:

- **Zero-width characters** — `U+200B`, `U+200C`, `U+200D`, `U+2060`, `U+FEFF`
- **Variation selectors** — `U+FE00` through `U+FE0F` (16 characters)
- **Bidirectional control marks** — `U+200E`, `U+200F`, `U+202A`–`U+202E`, `U+2066`–`U+2069`
- **Special & invisible spaces** — `U+00A0`, `U+202F`, `U+205F`, `U+2007`–`U+200A`
- **Invisible operators** — `U+00AD`, `U+2061`–`U+2064`
- **Line & paragraph separators** — `U+2028`, `U+2029`

When suspicious characters are found, you can optionally trigger a server-side **AI-generated content review** for a 0–100 confidence score and a short reason. The review is optional, fails gracefully to a front-end-only result, and never stores your input.

## Our values

1. **Privacy first.** Your text is scanned locally in your browser. It is only forwarded to our server when you explicitly trigger the AI review, and even then it is never stored.
2. **Free forever.** No credit card, no trial. Anonymous users get 5 checks per day; sign-up unlocks 50/day.
3. **Browser-first.** The core scan runs in your browser — no waiting on a remote model, no rate-limited API call for the basic use case.
4. **Open about limitations.** A negative result does not prove a text is human-written. A positive result does not prove a text is AI-generated. We tell you exactly what we found and let you decide what to do with it.
